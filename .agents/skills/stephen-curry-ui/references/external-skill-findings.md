# Research synthesis: external skills and independent adaptations

This is an **analysis of methods**, not bundled content from other vendors. Do not copy their code, screenshots, layouts, fonts, assets, examples, datasets, training material or private configuration into projects without independent licensing verification. No other skill is required at runtime.

Checked primary SKILL.md and selected supporting documents in November 2026; repository contents may change. All vendor claims, such as benchmark sizes, are project self-descriptions rather than independently audited results.

## Ranking by usefulness for this skill's objective

- **Whole-journey creative production:** Auteur, Website Analyzer, Awwwards Web Design.
- **Visual exploration:** Huashu, Baoyu, OIL (methods only).
- **Specialized effect knowledge:** Awwwards Motion, Awwwards 3D.
- **Truthful review:** Jury, Vercel guidelines, Auteur verification.

## The synthesized gap we target

The original skill must cover **all four dimensions simultaneously**: visual composition at any paused frame, interactive time-state behavior, continuity across scenes/routes, and device-appropriate executable performance. Neither impressive screenshots alone nor a passing lint command is sufficient.
