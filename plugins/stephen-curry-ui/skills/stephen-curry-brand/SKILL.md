# Brand DNA Intelligence

Use this skill when a brand decision is part of the work. For a focused UI change, preserve the existing identity and inspect only the relevant brand rules.

1. Audit the available logo, identity rules, typography, voice, imagery, and usage rights. Keep valuable approved assets for mature brands.
2. Derive positioning, audience, differentiators, emotional cues, non-goals, and signature expression from verified research. Label evidence, inference, and creative judgment separately.
3. Define the relevant type, color, imagery, motion, and voice principles. Avoid inventing company facts or implying unverified brand claims.
4. Recommend a coherent direction. Ask for approval only when an unresolved choice is consequential or authority is unclear; otherwise proceed within the user's brief and mark assumptions.
5. For a new identity or substantial rebrand, create a versioned `brand-dna.md` with sources, rights constraints, decisions, and approval status. A direct user approval is recordable; a status field alone is not consent.

Read `../stephen-curry-ui/references/design-governance.md` when establishing a system-wide direction.
